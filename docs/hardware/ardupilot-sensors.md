# ArduPilot Hardware Sensor Reference Catalog

English source. [Russian mirror](../ru/ardupilot-sensors.md).

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This catalog documents industry-standard, production-grade sensors used across the ArduPilot ecosystem (CubePilot, Holybro, Matek, CUAV, Mauch, Sensirion, u-blox, LightWare, Garmin, and Ainstein). Low-grade hobbyist breakout modules and uncalibrated clones are intentionally excluded.

The specifications below serve as the hardware reference for:

* **Aircraft profile sensor presets and the Sensors configuration tab:** selecting onboard and external sensors, mounting offsets, orientations, and realistic error envelopes (`Ideal sensors` versus `With specified errors`, as named in [PROJECT_SCOPE.md](../../PROJECT_SCOPE.md) [U13]).
* **Autopilot error ownership and parameter generation (TASK-087, TASK-089):** mapping physical sensor characteristics to MirageSITL sensor models (`packages/sensors/`) and ArduPilot `INS_*`, `BARO_*`, `COMPASS_*`, `GPS*_*` (for example `GPS1_TYPE`), `BATT_*`, `ARSPD_*`, and `RNGFND*_*` parameters. Parameter names and enum values in this catalog follow ArduPilot at the commit pinned by MirageSITL (`8ab6afde107a91df1b5f60ade8f333cb8a61b190`) [U20][U25][U26][U27].
* **Phase 12 sensor physics (TASK-114, TASK-115, TASK-116, TASK-117, TASK-118) and propulsion power monitoring (TASK-092, TASK-104):** providing datasheet-backed noise densities, bias instabilities, conversion delays, thermal drifts, and dynamic ranges in both manufacturer datasheet units and MirageSITL contract units (`RadS`, `Mps2`, `Pa`, `Gauss`, `M`, `Mps`, `ms`; all SI except `Gauss` for magnetic flux density).

## 2. Unit Conversions for MirageSITL Contracts

MirageSITL sensor interfaces ([`IdealSensors`](../../packages/sensors/src/ideal.ts) and [`SensorNoise`](../../packages/sensors/src/noise.ts)) operate in SI units, with magnetic flux density in Gauss. `IdealSensors` takes NED truth inputs and reports FRD body-frame IMU (specific force, angular rate) and magnetometer observations plus scalar barometer values (pressure in Pa, altitude in m, temperature in K) and airspeed dynamic pressure (Pa); it has no GNSS output. `SensorNoise` is frame-agnostic (one scalar per channel) [U4]. Exact defined values are written in full; derived values are rounded to 3 significant figures:

* **Angular rate (`RadS`):** $1\text{ dps (deg/s)} = \frac{\pi}{180}\text{ rad/s} \approx 0.0175\text{ rad/s}$; $1\text{ mdps}/\sqrt{\text{Hz}} \approx 1.75 \times 10^{-5}\text{ rad/s}/\sqrt{\text{Hz}}$ [U1].
* **Specific force (`Mps2`):** $1\text{ }g = 9.80665\text{ m/s}^2$ (standard gravity, exact by definition); $1\text{ }\mu g/\sqrt{\text{Hz}} = 9.80665 \times 10^{-6}\text{ m/s}^2/\sqrt{\text{Hz}}$ (exact) [U2].
* **Pressure (`Pa`):** $1\text{ hPa} = 1\text{ mbar} = 100\text{ Pa}$ (exact); $1\text{ psi} \approx 6.89\text{ kPa}$ [U2]. At ISA sea level ($p_0 = 101325\text{ Pa}$, $T_0 = 288.15\text{ K}$, $\rho_0 = 1.225\text{ kg/m}^3$, $g_0 = 9.80665\text{ m/s}^2$), the hydrostatic gradient is $\mathrm{d}h/\mathrm{d}p = -1/(\rho_0 g_0) \approx -0.0832\text{ m/Pa}$ ($\approx -8.32\text{ m/hPa}$) [U3]. Its magnitude grows with altitude, reaching about $-0.0843\text{ m/Pa}$ near 135 m ISA altitude.
* **Magnetic flux density (`Gauss`):** $1\text{ Gauss (G)} = 100\text{ }\mu\text{T} = 100{,}000\text{ nT} = 1000\text{ mG}$; $1\text{ }\mu\text{T} = 0.01\text{ G}$; $1\text{ nT} = 10^{-5}\text{ G}$ (all exact, from $1\text{ G} = 10^{-4}\text{ T}$) [U2].
* **Band-limited discrete RMS noise:** For a one-sided white noise density $N_d$ (per $\sqrt{\text{Hz}}$, the datasheet convention) filtered by a first-order low-pass pole at $f_c\text{ (Hz)}$ (`bandwidthHz` in [`SensorNoise`](../../packages/sensors/src/noise.ts)), the equivalent continuous-time post-filter RMS is $\sigma = N_d \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2} f_c} \approx 1.25 \, N_d \sqrt{f_c}$, valid for $f_c \ll f_s$. `SensorNoise` itself is a discrete first-order (AR(1)) filter with decay $a = e^{-2\pi f_c/f_s}$, requires $f_c < f_s/2$, and takes the post-filter RMS directly as its `rms` argument [U4]. A unity-DC-gain AR(1) filter with the same pole, $y[n] = a\,y[n-1] + (1-a)\,x[n]$, driven by sampled white noise of variance $N_d^2 f_s/2$, gives $\sigma = N_d \sqrt{\frac{1-a}{1+a} \cdot \frac{f_s}{2}}$, lower than the continuous formula by 1.60 % at $f_c = f_s/10$ and by 8.62 % at $f_c = f_s/4$ (recomputed). This deviation does not apply to `SensorNoise`'s own input gain, which is $\mathit{rms}\,\sqrt{1-a^2}$ on a unit Gaussian [U4].

### IMU Architecture Notes

* **Redundancy & Heating:** The three-IMU mix depends on the board revision. Cube Orange+ as specified by CubePilot carries ICM-42688-P, ICM-20948 and ICM-20649, with two IMUs vibration-isolated and the third fixed as a reference/backup [I14]; its ArduPilot hwdef also accepts a second ICM-42688-P and an ICM-45686 in place of ICM-20948 and ICM-20649 [I9]. Pixhawk 6X Rev 3/4 (discontinued) is heterogeneous (ICM-20649 or BMI088, ICM-42688-P, ICM-42670-P), Rev 8 carries three identical ICM-45686, and the 6X Pro carries ADIS16470 and IIM-42652 (isolated) plus a hard-mounted ICM-45686 [I15][I10]. ICM-20948, ICM-42670-P, ADIS16470 and IIM-42652 are not catalogued in this section. ArduPilot's accelerometer clip counter is at $15.5\text{ }g$ for ICM-42688-P and BMI088 and at $29.5\text{ }g$ for ICM-45686 and ICM-20649 [I6][I7][I8]. The manufacturers do not specify the isolation material. The heater is a closed-loop PI controller (`BRD_HEAT_P`, `BRD_HEAT_I`, `BRD_HEAT_IMAX`; heat only, 0–100 % duty) [I17]. `BRD_HEAT_TARG` defaults to $45^\circ\text{C}$ on the Cube Orange/Orange+, Pixhawk 6X, 6C, 5X and CUAV X7 hwdefs (global default $-1$ = disabled, range $-1$ to $80^\circ\text{C}$, and the firmware caps the target at $65^\circ\text{C}$) [I17][I9][I10][I11][I13]; the Matek H743 hwdef defines no heater [I12]. Heating reduces thermal bias drift but does not eliminate it: drift remains during warm-up, above the setpoint, and on IMUs away from the heater (the third IMU on Cube Orange), and ArduPilot treats the residual with IMU temperature calibration (`INS_TCAL1_*`..`INS_TCAL3_*`) [I18][I5].
* **In-Run Bias Stability:** Not specified by the manufacturer for any IMU in this table (DS-000347, DS-000489, DS-000192 and BST-BMI088-DS000-19 give no bias-instability or Allan-deviation figure) [I1][I2][I3][I4]. MirageSITL must not present an in-run bias instability value for these parts as datasheet-backed; use a measured Allan-variance value or label the value as an assumption.

## 4. Barometers (Static Atmospheric Pressure)

Relevant tasks: TASK-034, TASK-035, TASK-089, TASK-116.
ArduPilot parameters (pinned SITL commit `8ab6afde107a91df1b5f60ade8f333cb8a61b190`, identical to master for these files): `BARO1_DEVID` (internal-use device ID set by the driver, not a user setting) [B10], `BARO_PRIMARY` (`0`: FirstBaro, `1`: 2ndBaro, `2`: 3rdBaro; default `0`) [B10], `BARO_EXT_BUS` (`-1`: Disabled, `0`: Bus0, `1`: Bus1, `6`: Bus6; at `-1` the driver probes all external I2C buses per `BARO_PROBE_EXT`) [B10], `BARO1_WCF_ENABLE` (`0`: Disabled, `1`: Enabled; default `0`; built only when `HAL_BARO_WIND_COMP_ENABLED`, default `1`) [B10][B11], `GNDEFF_ALT` (m, range $-1\text{–}10$, default $0.5$; negative disables ground-effect compensation) and `GNDEFF_TMO` (s, range $0\text{–}5$, default $2$) [B12], `EK3_GND_EFF_DZ` (m, range $0\text{–}10$, default $4$) [B14]. `GND_EFFECT_COMP` (`0`: Disabled, `1`: Enabled; default `1`) exists in released Copter 4.7.x firmware (checked at Copter-4.7.0, 4.7.1 and 4.7.2, the latest 4.7 tag) [B13]; at the pinned commit it is retired, and when `AP_GROUNDEFFECT_ENABLED` is built a stored `0` is converted to `GNDEFF_ALT = -1` unless `GNDEFF_ALT` is already configured [B12].

## 10. Reference Flight Controller Board Sensor Suites

When offering board-level presets in the aircraft editor, the following combinations are example pairings of ArduPilot-supported hardware. The sensors fitted to a board depend on its hardware revision, as stated per item:

1. **CubePilot Cube Orange+ + Here3+ / Here4 + Mauch HS-100 (Industrial Multirotor / VTOL):**
   * **IMUs:** Three IMUs; the chip in each slot depends on the IMU-board revision and is detected by WHOAMI [U5][U7]: slot 0 (vibration-isolated) ICM-42688 or ICM-45686; slot 1 (vibration-isolated) ICM-20948, ICM-42688 or ICM-45686; slot 2 (fixed, not isolated) ICM-20649 (wide-range part) or ICM-45686. The original set is ICM-42688 + ICM-20948 + ICM-20649 [U6]. Temperature-regulated by an IMU heater with a default target of 45 °C (`BRD_HEAT_TARG`) [U5][U27].
   * **Barometers:** Dual TE MS5611 on SPI [U5][U6].
   * **Magnetometers:** External PNI RM3100 on DroneCAN in both the Here3+ [U8] and the Here4 [U9][U10]; internal AK09916 backup (the magnetometer inside the ICM-20948), present only when the ICM-20948 is fitted in slot 1 [U5][U6].
   * **GNSS:** u-blox NEO-F9P, L1/L5 RTK (Here4) [U9][U10] or u-blox M8P-2, single-band RTK (Here3+) [U8], via DroneCAN (`GPS1_TYPE=9`, DroneCAN) [U25]. Both GNSS units support CAN FD up to 8 Mbit/s [U8][U9]; ArduPilot runs the bus as classic CAN at 1 Mbit/s (`CAN_P1_BITRATE` default 1000000) unless bit 2 (EnableCanfd) of `CAN_D1_UC_OPTION` is set, in which case the Cube Orange+ data-phase default `CAN_P1_FDBITRATE` is 8 (8 Mbit/s) [U5][U29].
   * **Battery Monitor:** Mauch HS-100 with an Allegro Hall-effect current sensor (Mauch names it ACS-100U; ACS758-100U on its PL-100 sensor board) [U11][U12]. The Mauch HS-100 is an analog monitor: `BATT_MONITOR=4` (Analog Voltage and Current), scaled with `BATT_VOLT_MULT` and `BATT_AMP_PERVLT` [U11][U20]. Alternative: CubePilot Power Brick Mini; its sensing type and scaling are unverified, because no CubePilot specification was reached (only retailer and forum pages describe it as an analog voltage and current module).
2. **Holybro Pixhawk 6X + H-RTK F9P + PM02D + SDP3X (Commercial VTOL / Fixed-Wing):**
   * **IMUs:** Three IMUs (triple-redundant), vibration-isolated and heated (default target 45 °C, `BRD_HEAT_TARG`) [U14][U15][U16][U27]. The set depends on the revision [U14][U15]: Rev 3/4 (discontinued) ICM-42688-P + BMI088 (or ICM-20649 in the BMI088 position) + ICM-42670-P; Rev 8 (currently shipping) 3× ICM-45686, where ArduPilot also accepts an LSM6DSV-family part in each slot. Other 6X variants carry different sets: ArduPilot's Holybro 6X Rev 6 board ID probes ADIS16470 + IIM-42652 + ICM-45686 [U14], the same three IMUs that PX4 lists for the Holybro Pixhawk 6X Pro [U28], and the CUAV 6X has its own set [U14].
   * **Barometers:** Two, on separate buses [U15]: 2× BMP388 (Rev 3/4) or ICP-20100 + BMP388 (Rev 8) [U15]. ArduPilot also probes 2× BMP581 for more recent Holybro 6X versions [U14].
   * **Magnetometers:** Internal Bosch BMM150 (Holybro 6X) [U14][U15]; external iSentek IST8310 on the standard H-RTK F9P Rover and Helical [U17]. A PNI RM3100 is fitted only on Holybro's separate H-RTK ZED-F9P Rover (RM3100) and H-RTK NEO-F9P lines [U18].
   * **GNSS:** u-blox ZED-F9P (H-RTK F9P) [U17] or u-blox M9N (Holybro M9N GPS; NEO-M9N on the DroneCAN version) [U19].
   * **Battery Monitor:** Holybro PM02D digital I2C module: TI INA228 (current production) or TI INA226 (the out-of-production 6S version sold under the same name) [U21]. ArduPilot `BATT_MONITOR=21` (INA2XX), which is the Pixhawk 6X firmware default on I2C bus 1 [U14][U20].
   * **Airspeed:** Sensirion SDP31/SDP33: `ARSPD_TYPE=6` (I2C-SDP3X) when wired by I2C, or `ARSPD_TYPE=8` (DroneCAN) behind a DroneCAN airspeed node [U26].
3. **Matek H743-WING + Matek M10Q-5883 + MS5525DSO (Compact Fixed-Wing / Long-Range UAV):**
   * **IMUs:** Two IMUs (unheated: the firmware defines no IMU heater); the pair depends on the revision [U22]: V3 ICM-42688-P + ICM-42605; V1.5/V2 ICM-42605 + MPU6000; V1 ICM-20602 + MPU6000.
   * **Barometer:** Single onboard Infineon DPS310 on I2C2 [U22][U23]. MS5611 and BMP280 are probed only as external barometers [U22].
   * **Magnetometer:** No built-in compass [U22][U23]; external QST QMC5883L on the M10Q-5883 puck, on I2C [U24].
   * **GNSS:** u-blox M10-series receiver on the M10Q-5883 (the exact module is not confirmed by a Matek source reached here; retailer listings disagree between SAM-M10Q and MAX-M10S) [U24]; `GPS1_TYPE=2` (uBlox) [U25].
   * **Battery Monitor:** Onboard voltage divider and integrated current sensor (WING V2/V3) read by the ADC: `BATT_MONITOR=4` (Analog Voltage and Current), the firmware default [U20][U22]. The firmware default `BATT_AMP_PERVLT` is 40 A/V; ArduPilot documents 66.7 A/V for WING V2/V3 [U22][U23]. The current-sensing element (shunt or Hall) is not specified by the manufacturer in the sources reached.
   * **Airspeed:** TE Connectivity MS5525DSO (`ARSPD_TYPE=3`, or `4`/`5` to fix the I2C address at 0x76/0x77) or Sensirion SDP3X (`ARSPD_TYPE=6`) [U26].
